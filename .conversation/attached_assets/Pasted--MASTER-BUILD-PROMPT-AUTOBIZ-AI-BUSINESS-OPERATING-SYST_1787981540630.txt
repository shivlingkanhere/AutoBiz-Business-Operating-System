# MASTER BUILD PROMPT — AUTOBIZ AI BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM

You are a senior full-stack SaaS architect, product engineer, UI/UX designer, database architect, security engineer, and AI agent engineer.

Build a complete, scalable SaaS platform called **AutoBiz**.

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

AutoBiz is an AI-powered Business Operating System for small and medium-sized businesses.

It should help a business owner manage:

* Business information
* Products
* Categories
* Inventory
* Customers
* Suppliers
* Sales
* Payments
* Expenses
* Invoices
* Reports
* Business analytics
* AI business assistant
* Notifications
* Employees and permissions

The key differentiator is the AI assistant.

AutoBiz AI must NOT be a simple FAQ chatbot.

It must understand natural, incomplete, informal, typo-filled, multilingual and Hinglish/Marathi/English user inputs, identify the user's intent, understand conversation context, retrieve relevant business data, perform calculations/analysis, and provide useful business insights.

The long-term goal is:

DATA → UNDERSTANDING → ANALYSIS → INSIGHT → RECOMMENDATION → APPROVED ACTION

# 8. MULTI-TENANCY

This is extremely important.

AutoBiz is a multi-tenant SaaS.

Business A must NEVER be able to access Business B's data.

All business-owned records must be associated with a business_id.

Examples:

* products.business_id
* customers.business_id
* suppliers.business_id
* sales.business_id
* expenses.business_id
* inventory_transactions.business_id

Every backend query must enforce tenant isolation.

Do NOT rely only on frontend filtering.

Use authenticated user context to determine the business.

Implement reusable authorization/tenant middleware.
